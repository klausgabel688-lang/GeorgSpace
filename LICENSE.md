# Georg Space Commercial Plugin License — Summary

This summary applies to paid Georg Space Minecraft plugin files delivered through an official Discord purchase ticket.

## Granted rights

After confirmed payment, the buyer receives a non-exclusive license to install and use the purchased plugin on one Minecraft server network operated by the buyer.

## Not allowed

- Publishing or redistributing the plugin files
- Reselling, sharing, leaking or re-uploading the files
- Claiming the plugin or its assets as the buyer's own work
- Removing author or license information
- Giving the files to another server owner
- Publishing source obtained through decompilation

## Ownership

The purchase grants permission to use the delivered build. It does not transfer copyright, source code or ownership of the project.

## Dependencies and third-party content

Dependencies remain subject to their own licenses. Commercial packages include only assets and music that may legally be distributed. Third-party server plugins are not resold as Georg Space products.

## Support and compatibility

The supported Minecraft, Paper and Java versions are stated on the product page or confirmed in the purchase ticket. Installation help is provided for the purchased build. Major custom changes are separate development work.

## Refunds and withdrawal

Refunds, defects and any statutory withdrawal rights are handled according to applicable law and the agreement made in the purchase ticket. This summary does not remove rights that the law gives the buyer.

The final purchase ticket should record the product, version, price, buyer and delivery date.
